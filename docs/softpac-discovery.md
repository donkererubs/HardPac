# SoftPac discovery log

Everything we learn about SoftPac, its domain, and the client's needs.
Each entry: **date**, **source**, **fact/insight**. Keep it factual — analysis
and decisions belong in `decisions/`, not here.

---

## Open questions (start here in interviews)

### Product & domain
- [ ] What exactly does SoftPac do? (transport? warehousing? fleet? orders/invoicing?) — full feature landscape
- [ ] Who are the end users, and what are their daily workflows?
- [ ] What is the business domain vocabulary? (entities: shipments, pallets, routes, customers…?)

### Technical reality of SoftPac
- [ ] What platform is SoftPac today? (desktop app? client-server? hosted web?) — language/OS hints
- [ ] How is it deployed and maintained today?
- [ ] Does it integrate with external systems? (ERP, EDI, GPS/telematics, accounting…?)

### Why rewrite
- [ ] What are the top pain points / limitations driving the rewrite?
- [ ] What must the successor do *exactly the same* (parity requirements)?
- [ ] What new capabilities does the client dream about (the "why now")?

### Constraints
- [ ] Timeline, team, budget?
- [ ] Does the client own the data/schema we must migrate from?
- [ ] Regulatory or reporting obligations?
